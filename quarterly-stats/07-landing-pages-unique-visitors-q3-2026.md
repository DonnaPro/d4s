## What this explains

### The UK doubling has a cause, and it is new content

The country tab showed UK human sessions nearly doubling, 225 to 434, with no explanation. This table names it:

| Page | Q2 | Q3 |
|---|---|---|
| /comparison/best-virtual-assistant-companies-uk/ | 0 | **95** |
| /guides/how-to-hire-virtual-assistant-uk/ | 0 | **59** |
| /countries/uk/ | 13 | **32** |
| **Total UK-oriented first touches** | **13** | **186** |

Three pages account for 173 of the UK's 209-session gain. Two of them did not exist as landing pages in Q2 at all. **The UK growth is new comparison and guide content ranking**, not a general lift.

That is the template. `/comparison/best-virtual-assistant-companies-uk/` alone brought 95 unique visitors in its first quarter, more than `/pricing/`, `/countries/germany/` and `/countries/switzerland/` combined.

## Two data-quality problems found while building this

### 1. Tag Manager was inflating the numbers, and it affects a tab already delivered

`/render` appeared as the **second-largest landing page** at 297 visitors in Q3 and 393 in Q2. It is not a page on your site. It is `gtm-msr.appspot.com/render?id=gtm-nxqzh7jw`, Google Tag Manager's own measurement iframe firing PostHog pageviews from its own domain.

Every host sending pageviews into the project:

| Host | Sessions |
|---|---|
| donnapro.com | 55,690 |
| **gtm-msr.appspot.com** | **695** |
| donnapro-com.translate.goog | 47 |
| astro.donnapro.com | 26 |
| app.donnapro.com | 10 |
| wpbackup / cdn / lime .donnapro.com | 7 |

**Correction to the `Organic visitors by country` tab.** Its "All organic visitors (any page)" column counts sessions on any entry page, so it swept these in. The homepage column is unaffected, because none of this traffic enters on `/`.

| Column | As delivered | Corrected | Removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q2 all pages, bots excluded | 2,579 | **2,186** | 393 |
| Q3 all pages, bots excluded | 2,453 | **2,159** | 303 |
| Q2 homepage | 651 | 651 | 0 |
| Q3 homepage | 647 | 647 | 0 |

Note this also means your published Q2 figure of 3,050 was itself inflated by roughly 400 sessions, since the original method had no host filter either.

**Every future query on this project should carry `properties.$host = 'donnapro.com'`.** It is now in all the saved queries in this folder.

Worth separately checking why GTM fires a PostHog pageview at all. That is a tagging misconfiguration, not just a reporting nuisance.

### 2. A broken link is producing 404 landing pages

`/faq/)/` drew **10 unique visitors** in Q3 as a first touch. A stray closing bracket is being appended to URLs. The same pattern appears at `/pricing/)/`, `/blog/virtual-assistant-vs-remote-executive-assistant/)/` and `/get-started/confirmed/)/`.

These are real people landing on 404s from an external link, most likely a markdown link written as `[text](url))` somewhere off-site, or a malformed link in the Astro source. Worth grepping the repo for `/)` and checking which external pages link in this way.

## Reusable query

```sql
SELECT
  entry_path AS landing_page,
  uniqIf(pid, q = 'Q2') AS q2_unique_visitors,
  uniqIf(pid, q = 'Q3') AS q3_unique_visitors
FROM (
  SELECT
    $session_id AS sid,
    any(person_id)                                                              AS pid,
    argMin(properties.$geoip_country_code, timestamp)                           AS country,
    argMin(if(properties.$pathname = '', '/', properties.$pathname), timestamp) AS entry_path,
    argMin(properties.$host, timestamp)                                         AS entry_host,
    coalesce(any(session.$channel_type), '')                                    AS channel,
    coalesce(any(session.$entry_referring_domain), '')                          AS refdom,
    toDate(toTimeZone(min(timestamp), 'Europe/Ljubljana'))                      AS ds,
    max(coalesce(properties.$virt_is_bot, false))                               AS is_bot,
    countIf(properties.$pathname LIKE '/careers%'
         OR properties.$pathname LIKE '/bravo/careers%')                        AS careers,
    if(ds <= toDate('2026-06-30'), 'Q2', 'Q3')                                  AS q
  FROM events
  WHERE event = '$pageview'
    AND timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-03-31 00:00:00')
    AND timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-10-01 00:00:00')
    AND $session_id != ''
  GROUP BY sid
  HAVING ds >= toDate('2026-04-01') AND ds <= toDate('2026-09-30')
    AND entry_host = 'donnapro.com'
    AND careers = 0
    AND NOT is_bot
    AND channel NOT IN ('Organic Social','Paid Social','Organic Video','Paid Search')
    AND refdom NOT ILIKE '%donnapro%'
    AND country IN ('AT','BE','DK','FI','FR','DE','IE','LU','NL','NO','SE','CH','GB','AU','CA','AE','US')
)
GROUP BY landing_page
ORDER BY q3_unique_visitors DESC, q2_unique_visitors DESC
```

Swap `uniqIf(pid, ...)` for `countIf(...)` to get sessions instead of unique visitors.
